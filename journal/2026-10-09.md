# Project journal — 2026-10-09

## Decisions made today

- Build a containerized prototype with separate API and planner web app. Training, evaluation, and API calls must also work from a terminal.
- Keep runtime settings in a validated YAML file: `config/project.yaml`.
- Keep ETL, feature preparation, market mapping, splitting, training, evaluation, and model packaging as separate pipeline stages.
- Keep the supplied workbooks unchanged. Write processed tables to `artifacts/processed/` and versioned results to `artifacts/runs/<run_id>/`.
- Use a transparent seats-to-hotel conversion chain for scenarios. Compare seasonal, air-capacity-adjusted, regression, and XGBoost baselines. Select a more complex model only if the development-period result justifies it.
- Main modelling grain: month × hotel guest nationality. Flight records are prepared by month × departure origin, then joined through an explicit, uncertain market allocation.
- Scenario inputs: month, departure country/city, route status, frequency, seat capacity, load factor; visible estimates or overrides for transfer/transit, nonresident visitor, hotel conversion, and stay length.
- Observed primary training target: monthly hotel check-ins from `New Arrivals`. Secondary target: monthly guest-days from daily `Guests`. Do not call guest-days verified overnight guest nights until same-day treatment is checked.
- Internal train period ends 2024-12-31; back-test on 2025-01-01 through 2025-07-31. The official later test files hide `Guests` answers.
- Domestic hotel records remain a separate forecasting/context task, outside the first international flight-conversion model.
- The current files do not provide direct event/holiday, aircraft-type, length-of-stay, or guest-night fields. Do not present these as observed features.

## Why these choices

The challenge asks for both an auditable conversion chain and improved seasonal/market prediction. The available history is short for a deep model, and departure origin is not guest nationality. The transparent chain keeps scenario effects inspectable; benchmarks and held-out data determine whether a more flexible model adds value.

**Untouched holdout:** January–July 2025, 310 complete nationality-month rows. Hotel check-ins WMAPE: **18.82%**, MAE: **940.38 check-ins per row**, bias: **−4.84%**. Seasonal-only check-ins WMAPE: **22.24%**. Guest-days proxy WMAPE: **18.83%**, versus **20.16%** seasonal-only.

**Interpretation:** Scheduled air capacity added useful signal beyond last year's hotel demand. The model still tends to underpredict check-ins slightly. These errors measure baseline demand prediction, not the causal effect of adding a route. Scenario uplift and nationality allocation depend on visible assumptions. The reported guest-day metric is not verified overnight guest nights.

The API estimates about **386 additional hotel check-ins** and **1,392 additional guest nights**, subject to the displayed visitor, capture, stay, and nationality-allocation assumptions.
